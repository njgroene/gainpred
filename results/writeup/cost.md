# Cost of a round, by part

Generated by `uv run python -m gainpred.writeup` from the round logs; do not edit by hand.

Per teacher and round: mean over the runs (seconds on one A10 GPU; tokens in thousands). *Build* = the teacher building its kept answers; *replay* = easy problems answered by the model (addition only; longest run replays true answers); *checks* = P8 checks at every length; *train* = 3,000 updates. Check share = checks seconds as a share of build + replay + checks + train seconds. Evaluation on the test sets is not timed and not included. Generated tokens are what the model wrote; train tokens are answer tokens trained on (not generated).

Check tokens: exact for longest run. For addition the log sums replay and check tokens; the replay share is estimated at 165 thousand tokens a round (true-answer lengths, exact when the replay is answered 100% right) and subtracted.
