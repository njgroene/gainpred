# Steered rounds (amendment 25)

**Read this first.** The arms "novelty at least 3%" and "rule as frozen" below left skipped lengths out of training altogether. That was an error in the design of the experiment (see amendment 26): the checks say that teaching a learned length adds nothing, not that it can be left out. The collapses reported here are catastrophic forgetting (McCloskey and Cohen, 1989), which rehearsal prevents (Robins, 1995). The arm "with rehearsal" is the pilot for the experiment of amendment 26, written up in `results/writeup/steer_rehearsal.md`.
