## Decisions I took

Where the spec left a choice open, I took the reading closest to its exact definitions. No definition,
threshold, split or number was changed.

1. **"Learned" for the answer key** (two repeats, change A): both repeats must reach 90% at every length
   of the floor ("at least two of three" read as "all of two").
2. **Where the two "seen coming" numbers are computed**: once per version of floor 1 and repeat, inside
   that version's agreement-rule run (`seen=True`), before its first floor-2 round. The no-carry run of
   the same version and repeat starts from the identical model, so the numbers are the same for both
   teachers and are not computed twice.
3. **Pilot spending** is the logged wall time of each Modal call (`call_done` / `call.json`) at $1.10
   an hour on the A10, from the first pilot call (timing) on. A call that did not finish counts with the
   seconds its finished rounds logged.
4. **Cost estimates for the cap** use the timing measurement's 23-digit generation rate at every length:
   at 31 and 39 digits the solid model stops early, so those timings were too low to trust. On stage B
   the estimates came out about 7% above the measured time, so they were used unscaled.
5. **Seed arguments that change with the repeat** (answer 13): every random stream is seeded by
   sha256 of "pilot | starting model | repeat | floor | round | digits | purpose", so the repeat number
   changes the fresh problems drawn for checks and building, the order of training batches
   (purpose "batches") and the solver's sampling seed (purpose "solver"). The starting model, the test
   problems, the floor-1 rehearsal set, the splits and the corruption function do not depend on the
   repeat. The same repeat in two conditions draws the same problems.
6. **Under-trained floor-1 runs** copy the starting model's config and checkpoint into
   `/vol/runs/pilot/<run>-start` so that every pilot file stays under `/vol/runs/pilot/`; the
   training code itself is the study's, unchanged.
7. **The rehearsal set is not filtered**: its wrong answers stay in training, and the audit counts them.
8. **Floor-1 novelty** uses the study's teacher with trusted pieces (no-carry rule, pieces of 3-7
   digits), so it covers only problems with no carry at cut 1; this is stated beside every table of it.
9. **Under-trained choice**: "average accuracy over those eight lengths" is the mean over 8-15 of the
   mean of the carry and no-carry groups, with 13-15 from the five-cut set (answer 12). A tie would go to
   fewer updates.
